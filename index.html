Hero - The case for decentralized systems

Decentralization can function with resilient, responsible, self-aware participation if the nodes (people and machines) themselves remain grounded and accountable. In 2026 we find outselves in a centralized culture that is rapidly deteriorating human life, it's the only possible and necessary step towards potential total autonomy for people in the long run.

Decentralized systems allow for more modest and equally spread participation and contribution. There is increased resilience since there is no single top-level point of failure, such as in centralized systems. There is more emphasis on local autonomy which in practice, returns freedom (through influence) and accompanying responsibility back to the people.

This infrastructue is applicable to collectives such as NGOs, small organizations, academic institutions, schools and individuals such as artists and creators.

The hero of this endeavor is namely you - the user.

Architecture of trust

Universe.si is built around a simple principle: technology should serve the user, not capture them. The infrastructure is designed with:
  • Data ownership - your files remain yours.
  • Transparency - clear information about how the service operates.
  • Privacy by design - only the data necessary to provide the service is handled.
  • User autonomy - you can access, export, or leave with your data at any time.
  • Responsible maintenance - backups, updates, and support are handled without removing your control.
Trust is not created by promises, but by giving users visibility, ownership, and freedom of choice.

Exit

You may leave the service at any time on your own terms and permanently close your account. Upon request, you have the right to:
  • Access the personal data stored about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  • Request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten"), where applicable.
  • Export your data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format (data portability).
  • Restrict or object to processing of your personal data where applicable.
  • Withdraw consent for processing based on consent.
  • Receive information about how your data is processed and stored.
Account closure will result in the removal or anonymisation of personal data according to the applicable retention requirements.
